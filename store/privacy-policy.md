# A11yScope Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

## Short version

A11yScope does not collect, transmit, or store your data. Scans run entirely
inside your browser.

## What the extension accesses

When you click **Scan this page**, A11yScope reads the structure and computed
styles of that one page in order to test it against WCAG success criteria. This
is the same information already rendered in your browser.

It uses the `activeTab` permission, which grants access only to the tab you
invoked it on, only at the moment you invoked it. It does not have standing
access to your browsing, and it cannot read pages you have not scanned.

## What leaves your browser

Nothing. There is no server component, no account, no login, no telemetry, and
no analytics. Scan results exist only in the side panel until you close it.

If you export a report, the file is written directly to your computer by your
browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.

## What is stored locally

A single preference record in `chrome.storage.local`, holding your entitlement
status (free or Pro). No page content, no URLs, and no scan history are stored.

## Third parties

None. The extension bundles no third-party code and contacts no external
services.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of the above — for example if a cloud feature is
added — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change
will be described in the release notes.
